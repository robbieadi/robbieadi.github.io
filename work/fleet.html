Fleet, a delivery platform from scratch

Fleet is the delivery management system Stamps offers to merchants. I was the primary developer and built it from the ground up: the backend, the backoffice, the courier integrations, and work on the rider app.

Scope
Backend, backoffice, rider app
Couriers
GoSend, Grab Express, JNE, in-house riders
Rider app
Fleet Rider App
Built with
Django, Kotlin, Firebase

01The problem

Merchants needed orders delivered, either by their own riders or by third-party couriers. That needed one place to model a delivery from creation to completion, whoever carries it.

02The core: a delivery task

Everything in Fleet hangs off a task with an explicit lifecycle, and every status change is logged:

create→ confirm→ dispatch→ track→ complete / cancel

In-house riders and third-party couriers move through the same lifecycle.

03What I built

Courier integrations

  • GoSend: asynchronous booking, webhook status processing, driver-search monitoring, driver tracking, live tracking URLs, cancellation.
  • Grab Express: delivery preview, webhook processing, auth token management.
  • JNE.

Bookings are made asynchronously, and status updates come back through webhooks and move the task through its lifecycle.

In-house riders

  • Rider authentication, password management, and assignment of tasks to riders.
  • FCM push notifications to riders.
  • Proof of pickup and proof of delivery.
  • On the rider app, location updates sent only while a delivery is in progress.

Fees and tracking

  • Distance-based delivery fee calculation, with radius validation and configurable delivery variants.
  • Live tracking URLs.

Backoffice

  • Delivery task management, rider management, reporting, and AWB download.

04Result

Fleet runs as a Stamps product: one delivery model behind both in-house riders and third-party couriers, with a rider app on Google Play.

It's the largest system I've built from scratch.
