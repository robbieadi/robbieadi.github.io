Selected work

Problem, constraint, decision, result

  • 01

    A payment terminal with no SDK →

    BCA handed over protocol documentation and nothing else. I wrote the wire format, USB serial I/O and polling loop so a self-service kiosk could take QRIS and card payments.

     USB serial
  • 02

    Keeping open orders in sync across tills →

    Real-time open-order sync between POS terminals over Firestore, for an app built to work alone and offline. Revision IDs filter out stale updates and echoes, and closed sales can never be overwritten.

     Firestore
  • 03

    Rebuilding a POS while it takes payments →

    MVP to MVVM, XML to Jetpack Compose, Realm to Room. Done one piece at a time, still in progress, in an app that never stops processing transactions.

     Compose · Room
  • 04

    One contract for every receipt printer →

    Thermal printers from several vendors over Bluetooth, USB and network behind one interface. Supporting new hardware means implementing it, not adding another branch.

     Hardware
  • 05

    Logs for bugs nobody could reproduce →

    An on-device log of data changes, network traffic and user actions in five rotating 5MB files, zipped and uploaded on demand or after a crash. I designed it and led the juniors who built it. Operations later used it to flag suspicious user activity, which is not what it was designed for.

     25MB rotating
  • 06

    Fleet, a delivery platform from scratch →

    I built the dispatch platform and worked on the rider app that talks to it: task lifecycle, rider assignment, distance-based fees, live tracking, and third-party courier integrations.

     Backend + Android
  • 07

    Reports, 190x faster →

    Three report generators moved from Python-level loops to database-level operations. 41.91s became 0.22s.

     190x · 57x · 53x

Measured improvements

Before and after

−84% worst frame
Tab switching on the POS order list: worst frame 257.8 → 40.7ms on a tablet, and 334.9 → 204.9ms on a Sunmi T2 running Android 7.1, the app's minimum. Redundant list rebuilds per tab switch dropped from 4 to 1. Measured with gfxinfo framestats over 9 interleaved runs per variant (Mann-Whitney U, p < 0.05). Navigating away and back showed no significant change, and I reported that too.
−19% render time
The new-order screen of the POS, benchmarked across repeated lifecycle runs: 19% faster overall render, 22% faster view inflation, 23% faster resume.
26 → 14 min
CI build time, about 46% shorter, after moving to setup-gradle v4 with proper Gradle caching.
